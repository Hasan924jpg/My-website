Jamea Ahmadia Sunnia Kamil Madrasah Secures 2nd Place Nationwide in Fazil Honours Examination 2023

Under the Islamic Arabic University, Dhaka, Jamea Ahmadia Sunnia Kamil Madrasah has achieved remarkable success in the Fazil Honours Examination 2023, held in the year 2023. According to the published results, Jamea secured 2nd place nationwide in Fazil Honours for 1st, 2nd, and 3rd years, and 3rd place in Fazil Honours for 4th year.

In response to this outstanding achievement, Professor Abul Mohsin Muhammad Yahia Khan, Chairman of the Governing Body, along with all members of the governing body, the Honorable Principal Allama Hafiz Qazi Abdul Alim Razvi, and the entire teaching faculty, have expressed their heartfelt gratitude. They have also expressed firm determination to maintain and further this streak of success in the future.
