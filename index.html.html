About Jamea

History of Jamea Ahmadia Sunnia Kamil Madrasah

Year 1954. In Greater Chittagong, there was a silent procession to stymie the Prophet's lovers. The inactivity of saints haters. In such a transitional period, due to the need of time, Qutbul Auliya Hazratul Allama Alhaj Hafeez Qari Syed Ahmad Shah Sirikoti (R.H.) established Jamea Ahmadia Sunnia Alia in the Sholashahar of Chittagong based on Maslake Ala Hazrat. Since its inception, for more than half a century, this religious educational institution has been making a great contribution to the service of religion, nation and madhhab in the subcontinent, crossing the borders of the country. Under the patronage of the worthy successor of the founder, Allama Syed Muhammad Tayyab Shah (R.A.) and the present descendants Huzur Qiblah Maddazilu-hul Ali, the Jamea's fame is world-wide today. This is as bright as the sun.

Mission and Vision

The main aim of Jamea Ahmadia Sunnia Kamil Madrasah is to produce competent citizens and capable scholars of the religion by teaching the books written by the National Curriculum and Textbook Board (NCTB) approved by the Bangladesh Madrasa Education Board (BMEB) and the syllabus of the Islamic Arabic University (IAU) as well as the internal books of the Madrasah: Quran Majeed, Tafsir Sharif, Hadith Sharif, Fiqh, Balaghat, Mantiq, Arabic, Urdu, Persian language and literature and Fatawa-Faraiz. To spread the Aqeeda-e-Ahle-Sunnah, Maslake A'la Hazrat, Madhhab-e-Hanafi, Qaderiya in Tariqah and to spread the eternal principles and ideals of Islam.

Infrastructure

At a Glance

Based on the ideology of Iman-e-Ahle Sunnat Ala Hajrat. Sayedul Aulia Sayed Ahmad Shah Sirikoti (R) uttered a most valuable verdict at the time of the foundation laying ceremony of Jamea-“Yea Madrasah Ka Buniad Masalke Ala-Hajrat Per Dhala Geya”. So, Jamea started its onward journey with Hajrat Allamah Wakar Uddin Brelavi, a renowned Alem-e-deen of this sub continent and one of the pillars of Maslke Ala Hajrat in 1956. Then Shahebjada Gause Zaman Sayed Muhammad Tyeb Shah (R.) selected Allamah Nasarullah Khan, a note-worthy personality among the followers of the ideology of Hajrat. Therefore all the academic and administrative activities of Jamea had been being performed according to the maslake Ala Hajrat in the practical sense. This Jamea is the first institution that attempted to establish the doctrine of Ala Hajrat in Bangladesh and now undoubtedly plays the principal role in leading Maslak-e-Ala Hajrat around Bangladesh under the Principal.
