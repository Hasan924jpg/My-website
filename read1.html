Jamea Ahmadia Sunnia Kamil Madrasah Excels in the National Children and Youth Cultural Competition 2025

On Monday, November 3, 2025, the Islamic Foundation Bangladesh, Chattogram Division organized the National Children and Youth Cultural Competition 2025 at Haji Camp, Pahartali, Chattogram.

In this prestigious event, 28 students from Jamea Ahmadia Sunnia Kamil Madrasah participated, with 26 winning prizes in various categories. This outstanding performance earned Jamea the honor of being recognized as the Best Institution at the Metropolitan Level.

After the competition, the award-winning students met the Chairman of the Governing Council and the Principal to share their success and receive blessings.

Professor Abul Mohsin Md. Yahya Khan, Principal Kazi Abdul Alim Rezvi, Dr. Muhammad Liaqat Ali, and other esteemed faculty members congratulated the students for their remarkable achievement and expressed their determination to uphold this legacy of excellence in future competitions.
