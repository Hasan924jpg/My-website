Jamea Ahmadia Sunnia Kamil Madrasah Secures First Place Nationwide in Fazil (Pass) Examination 2023

The results of the Fazil (Pass) 1st, 2nd, and 3rd year examinations under the Islamic Arabic University Dhaka were published on May 13. According to the university's announcement, Jamea Ahmadia Sunnia Kamil Madrasah, Chittagong, secured first place in the 2nd and 3rd years and second place in the 1st year in the Fazil (Pass) Examination 2023 across the country.
